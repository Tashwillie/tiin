About the programme

One of the most important, and misunderstood, sectors in UK property

Many delegates arrive here after years in traditional property. Buy-to-let portfolios, HMOs, serviced accommodation, holiday lets: strategies that promised passive income but, for most, meant rising effort, shifting regulation, escalating costs and tightening margins.

This Mastermind was created for investors who want something different: a model built on certainty rather than constant change, and a way to create profit with purpose. When structured correctly, children's residential care is a sector where long-term stability, strong contractual income and real social impact sit side by side. This is not about running care services. It's about understanding how property, regulation, commissioning and ethics intersect, and how to structure long-term passive investments that improve outcomes for children.

Over 12 months we guide you from little or no exposure to children's homes to becoming a confident, credible and responsible investor in this space.

The Five Principles

The framework we build on. Every deal, every decision and every module of the programme is anchored to these five principles.

1.

Care Comes First

Children's welfare sets the standard. Rigorous care-provider selection and due diligence before anything else.

2.

Verify Demand

Validate the area. Understand commissioning need and how to work with local authorities.

3.

Financial Viability

Confirm the numbers work. Deal analysis, care-cost models and provider affordability.

4.

Regulation & Compliance

Know the regulatory landscape fully: Ofsted, planning and property requirements.

5.

Legal & Lease Structuring

Stable, secure leases structured to protect all parties over the long term.

Meet your programme leads

Two practitioners, equal footing, one standard

Louis Stedman-Bryce

Investment · Regulation · Public Policy

Louis's background spans property investment, deal structuring and public policy, including time as a Member of the European Parliament. That experience, combined with his personal lived experience of the care system, shapes both his approach to this sector and his drive to make profound change within it. Through Inkfish Group he has spent close to six years structuring and developing children's-home investments, working directly with care providers and local authorities, and now delivers specialist care models through Inkfish Homes. He understands how regulation is written, how it's enforced, and where investors most commonly come unstuck.

"At fifteen, I was placed in what would now be classified as an illegal children's home. Thirty-five years later, children are still being placed in similar settings. This work is personal, and the standards are not negotiable."

Carly Houston

Ethical Investment · Business Strategy · Mentoring

Carly started her property journey with a simple question: can I create financial security for my family and help people at the same time? Over 15 years she has built a sizeable portfolio, primarily in the supported-housing sector, delivering high-quality, specialist provision. Her experience spans HMOs and flat conversions through to boutique hotel developments. She has worked extensively with private investors, built on strong relationships and long-term structures, and has spent over a decade coaching and mentoring investors to create property businesses that are genuinely aligned with their values.

"Operating in line with your values and having a clear purpose for your business is just as important as having the financial clarity and strategy to make it sustainable."

Is this for you?

An honest filter, not a sales qualifier

A small cohort only works when everyone in it is the right fit. Read both columns honestly before you apply.

This programme is for you if

  • You're ready to commit to a 12-month journey, not a quick win
  • You value long-term sustainability over speculative, fast returns
  • You want to invest responsibly, with care and compliance built in
  • You're serious about entering this sector with the right structure and partners

It's not for you if

  • You're focused on high-volume speculation or transactional investing
  • You want speed and scale ahead of standards and care
  • You're expecting guaranteed numbers or pressure tactics
  • You want to become a care operator rather than a responsible investor
